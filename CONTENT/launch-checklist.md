# Pre-launch checklist

- Confirm legal company/trading name and registration details.
- Confirm Frank approves the “twins” brand story for public use.
- Confirm exact Cape Town service radius / suburbs.
- Add a business email address.
- Confirm working hours if they will be displayed.
- Replace all AI concept project/gallery images with verified real project photographs, or clearly label them as illustrative.
- Connect contact form to the chosen email/backend/form service.
- Add Privacy Policy and Terms if collecting form data.
- Add Google Business Profile link when available.
- Add Facebook / Instagram links when available.
- Compress real images to WebP/AVIF and provide correct alt text.
- Test phone and WhatsApp links on mobile.
- Add LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness structured data after business details are verified.
- Create Open Graph and social sharing image.
- Verify title/meta descriptions, sitemap, robots.txt and canonical URLs.
- Run Lighthouse mobile checks before launch.
