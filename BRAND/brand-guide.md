# TwinFinish mini brand guide

**Name:** TwinFinish Painting & Tiling  
**Founder:** Frank Sbu Biyela  
**Phone / WhatsApp:** 063 499 7520  
**Market:** Painting and tiling in Cape Town and surrounding areas  

The visual identity uses deep navy for trust and professionalism, vivid orange for energy and conversion points, and clean white/neutral surfaces for a premium contemporary feel. The current mark combines the S&S initials, house form, paint roller and tile motif.

Use the full logo for primary brand moments, and the cropped mark/favicon for small UI contexts. Keep clear space around the mark and avoid recolouring it into unrelated palettes.
